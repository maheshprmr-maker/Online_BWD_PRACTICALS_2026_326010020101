About the Department

The Department of Computer Science provides quality education in computing, programming, software development, and emerging technologies. It encourages innovation, research, and practical learning among students.

Our Vision

To become a center of excellence in computer science education and research, producing skilled professionals who contribute to society through technological innovation.

Our Mission

  1. Provide quality education in computer science and technology.
  2. Develop technical, analytical, and problem-solving skills.
  3. Promote research, creativity, and innovation.
  4. Encourage ethical practices and lifelong learning.
